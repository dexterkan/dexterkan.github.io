01 / FOLLOW THE DATA

Protect the regions that still matter.

A person can be privacy-sensitive and essential to the detection task. Removing that person entirely would also remove useful information.

What leaves the local device?

Non-sensitive image chunks and masked intermediate features from sensitive chunks. Edge servers execute their assigned portions of the downstream task.

Metric definitions and experimental setup

SSIM: structural similarity between sensitive input regions and an adversary’s reconstruction. Lower reconstruction similarity indicates less leakage under this attack model; it is not a formal privacy guarantee.

Utility degradation: (original mAP50 − obfuscated mAP50) / original mAP50. A reported loss of 14.99% means relative utility retention of 85.01%, not an absolute mAP50 of 85.01%.

Setup: COCO; people, dogs, and cats designated sensitive. PyTorch 2.4.1, CUDA 12.1. Local execution: RTX 3070 desktop; edge server: RTX A6000. Both target models use a ResNet-18 backbone split after Residual Layer 1. These are GPU measurements, not measurements on a phone or embedded IoT device.

Timing: local obfuscation + server-side target-model execution. Network transmission latency is excluded (Section V-F).

04 / IMPLEMENTATION & SCOPE

Three modules, one systems tradeoff.

Implementation

YOLOv8n-based sensitive-object detection with privacy-aware loss terms and knowledge distillation; resource-aware image partitioning and assignment; split ResNet-18 features with a U-Net-based mask generator trained against a proxy reconstruction adversary.

Alternating training balances downstream task loss with reconstruction resistance. Non-sensitive regions avoid unnecessary obfuscation.

Evaluation scope

Privacy is evaluated empirically against reconstruction attacks. Private training, formal privacy guarantees, and communication-aware latency optimization are outside the evaluated scope.

The explorer presents aggregate experimental results. Reconstruction similarity, task utility, and runtime are evaluated using the protocols documented above.
