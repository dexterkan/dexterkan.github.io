## Research overview

My dissertation research addresses the efficiency, privacy, and deployment constraints of machine learning on resource-constrained devices.

- **Efficient distributed execution:** TODORA, an online mechanism for offloading and resource allocation for time-sensitive vehicular tasks.
- **Privacy-aware distributed inference:** LLPRI, a vision inference pipeline balancing privacy, task utility, and latency.
- **Deployment-faithful model adaptation:** cloud–edge hardware-aware neural architecture search that adapts models to local data and changing device resources.
- **Collaborative LLM inference:** confidence- and hardness-aware speculative decoding with selective remote verification.
